# Surface: Homepage (`/`) and shared site shell

**Mode:** Persuade (homepage). The shell (nav, footer, page headers) carries into Operate pages: scanner, assistant, donate, explore, centres, dashboards.

**Audience and job:** a household member in the Kalyan–Dombivli pilot area with an item in hand who wants to know which bin it goes in, whether it's dangerous, or who'd take it. Secondary: society coordinators and NGOs evaluating the platform.

**Action:** upload a waste photo (primary); donate an item (secondary).

**Proof/content:** the eight waste categories and where each goes; how it works; why separating matters (no statistics); clearly labelled demo donation listings. No real partners, data or documents exist yet, so nothing is claimed.

**Constraints:** white ground, forest-green accent, soft neutral surfaces, rounded corners, no unnecessary animation (brief pins). Server-rendered HTML, no JS app code, mobile and desktop equal.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the segregation system itself. Every item belongs in a coloured, stencilled lane, and the homepage shows those lanes as a row of bins under a chute where you drop a photo. It refuses the category default of a centred eco headline over a row of icon cards, leaves and recycling arrows.

OWN-WORLD: White ground and ink #14211a, neutral surfaces #f2f4f1. The lanes take municipal bin colours: wet forest green #1f6b3a (also the brand accent), dry blue, e-waste slate, hazardous red, biomedical yellow, textile plum, reuse orange, unsure grey. Big Shoulders Stencil uppercase sets headlines and lane names, Mukta sets body text. Moulded-plastic colour fields with rounded tops, painted lane lines as dividers, and a stencil-bridged ring as the mark.

STORY: The visitor sees that every item has a correct lane, believes RELOOP AI will name the lane honestly and say when it's unsure, and drops a photo or lists an item to donate.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav. A full-width stencil headline "Don't Throw It Away. Give It a Better Way." at the largest display size, with the tagline beneath. A wide chute panel holds the photo upload field, an "Identify this item" button and a "Donate an item" link. Directly under it, eight bin lanes span the full width, each a link to that lane's guidance. On phones the lanes fold into a 4×2 grid.

Carried disciplines: certainty shown by shape, not colour (filled/half/hollow); one fixed red hazard strip, identical everywhere; every result line traces back to its source; listing statuses stamp on and never vanish. Signature interaction: choosing a photo fills the chute (file name shown) and the lanes stay the exits. No motion beyond one press-down on lanes.
